Privacy

Last updated 6 November 2026.

What Present stores

  • Your account: an email address or an Apple or Google sign-in, and the name you choose.
  • What you add: video clips, photos, drawings and notes, and the vlogs made from them.
  • Your timezone, so presents arrive at 7am your time, and a device token so we can send notifications.
  • If you connect with someone, who you are connected with.

How it is used

Only to make and deliver your vlogs. Each night your day's clips are transcribed and edited automatically; the editing uses Anthropic's Claude AI to choose moments and write captions. Your vlog is shown to you, and to the one person you connected with, if any. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads or tracking.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase in London. Notifications are sent through Apple. If you sign in with Apple or Google, they confirm who you are; we only receive your email and name.

Deleting your data

Raw clips are tidied away after they have been edited. To delete your account and everything in it, email tristanmiller.design@gmail.com and it will be removed.
